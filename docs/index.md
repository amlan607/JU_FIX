# JU_FIX Documentation

JU_FIX digitises the Jahangirnagar University Medical Centre. It replaces paper
registers and physical queues with a single web application used by students,
faculty and staff, doctors, pharmacists and administrators.

## The five roles

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| Student | Books appointments, reads their own record, downloads prescriptions and certificates |
| Faculty or Staff | The same clinical rights as a student |
| Doctor | Runs consultations, writes records and prescriptions, decides certificate requests |
| Pharmacist | Verifies and dispenses issued prescriptions |
| Admin | Approves registrations, manages accounts, reads operational reports |

An administrator manages accounts and operations. They deliberately have **no**
access to clinical content: diagnoses, prescriptions and certificate reasons stay
with the clinicians who created them.
